# Delivery: what we own in every offer

Source: the second-batch RFPs and bios, plus every buyer reply and review so far (`work/all_feedback.txt`,
2026-10-29). Evidence is quoted. Companion to `team/tech/TECH_SCOPE.md`: tech owns the architecture, we own
**who delivers, when, how it is run, and on what terms**.

## 1. Our responsibility

| # | Responsibility | Why (evidence) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | **Named team with allocation, certifications, languages and a verified start date**, consistent with `ROSTER.md` | Won decks: "named staff, daily allocations" (Enfuce), "exact engineer certifications and years of experience" (Helsinki). Every RFP in batch 2 asks for team profiles and certifications |
| D2 | **Mobilisation: start fast** | Enfuce won on "both named engineers are ready to start on the first of next month". Helen: "forcing us to wait until mid-month… drags down our momentum". A buyer liked "someone ready to plug into our data pipelines almost immediately" |
| D3 | **Delivery plan with gates and freeze windows** | Helsinki won on a "source-risk probing phase with a formal go/no-go decision gate". VR and Vahterus: "no production changes during Q1" |
| D4 | **Lean governance: written updates, no status-meeting overhead** | Aktia: "crusade against calendar-clogging status syncs". A winner "spares my calendar from pointless recurring status syncs". A "lean pair… avoids the heavy coordination overhead" |
| D5 | **Language and on-site commitments** | Finnish and Swedish runbooks "met" (Aktia). "On site in Espoo… entirely in Finnish… keeps workshops moving". VR bio: foreign-led workshops bogged down railway concepts |
| D6 | **Commercial-delivery terms**: replacement, recruitment, contract deviations, billing control | Aktia: binding "key-person substitution terms… refreshing accountability". Helsinki: accepted the standard agreement "with zero deviations". UPM: "billing drifted past initial department estimates" |
| D7 | **Handover and ownership by the client's own people** | Helsinki: "pairing directly with our internal developers and delivering native Finnish runbooks" |
| D8 | **Effort basis (person-days per phase) for the price owner** | Metsä: "submissions tended to anchor tightly to published figures rather than reflecting independent delivery calculations" |

**Not ours:** architecture and tech choices (tech role), the final price (price owner), storyline and visual style
(deck-writer). We hand over two slides per deck plus an effort basis.

## 2. Delivery patterns

| Id | Pattern | Trigger | Standard answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DP1 | Verified availability | "verified availability", "assigned specialist" | Name, %, start date, "confirmed against our resourcing plan"; never one person at 100% in two decks |
| DP2 | Freeze-aware plan | "no production changes in Q1", quarterly close | Build and test during the freeze in non-production; cut-over windows named outside it |
| DP3 | Billing control | timesheet friction, budget drift | Fixed monthly fee or hard cap; weekly hours report in the client's own tool; no overage without written approval |
| DP4 | Zero-deviation contract | standard purchase agreement, tracked changes | "We accept your terms as is; no tracked changes" |
| DP5 | Recruitment clause | right to recruit after 12 months | Accept explicitly, no fee |
| DP6 | Replacement guarantee | key-person risk | Equal seniority within 10 working days, 2-week overlap at our cost |
| DP7 | Written governance | hates meetings, cautious, audit | Bi-weekly written update + one monthly steering; decisions logged |
| DP8 | Language | Finnish/Swedish operations, foreign-led workshops | Native-language team, docs and workshops in the client's languages |
| DP9 | Regulated sourcing | FIN-FSA, EBA, DORA | DORA ICT third-party clauses: audit rights, register of information, exit plan, no unapproved subcontracting, EU-only processing |
| DP10 | ROI traceability | ROI-obsessed, monitors commits | Every change and commit linked to a backlog item with a stated € benefit; monthly benefit ledger |

## 3. Learning rule

After every verdict, add any delivery item the review rated `met` or `not addressed` to §2 with its quote. The
roster is the single source of truth for who is promised where: update `ROSTER.md` before posting a deck.
