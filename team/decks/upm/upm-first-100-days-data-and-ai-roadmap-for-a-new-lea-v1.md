# Proposed solution: from assessment to a roadmap your leadership can sign

| Phase | Days | What you get | Accepted when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Assess** | 1–30 | Capability assessment of the Enterprise Data & Analytics team across SAP, Snowflake + dbt, Databricks and the BI tool: skills, ownership, pipeline health | Leadership accepts the assessment report |

# Governance: every prioritised AI use case carries the same four controls

| Control | What it means in the roadmap |
|---|---|
| **Confidence scoring** | Every model recommendation shows a confidence score and top-3 ranked candidates. Threshold per use case, set with the business owner |
| **Source lineage** | Every output traces to its source tables: SAP S/4HANA, Snowflake models, dbt tests. Lineage shown next to the recommendation |
| **Human sign-off criteria** | A named person approves before any action. Criteria written per use case: what is auto-accepted, what is reviewed, what is rejected |
| **Cautious rollout** | Pilot, review, then scale. Nothing goes live during restructuring without leadership sign-off |

| Assistants | **Frontline mill assistant** | **Enterprise chat** |
|---|---|---|
| **Users** | Operators and maintenance, grounded in mill procedures | Office staff, broad questions |
| **Our view** | **Rank first:** narrow, testable, grounded | Later, after access rules are agreed |
