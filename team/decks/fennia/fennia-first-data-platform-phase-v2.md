# Solution: your blueprints, your Data Vault, your specification sheets

:::cols
### What we build
- **Binding blueprints implemented as given.** Gaps are raised as change requests to you, never silently redesigned.
- **Data Vault on your in-house warehouse:** hubs (policy, claim, party, coverage), links and satellites, loaded in increments and tested for key and link integrity
- **Statutory-figure lineage:** every figure in the BI report traces to raw policy and claims records in the insurance core system, answering the audit inquiries
- **BI reports from your specification sheets:** each field and calculation rule becomes a tested rule with a reference back to the sheet
:::col
### Out of scope
- No redesign of the architecture
- No new platform or licences; your public cloud, warehouse and BI tool only
- No changes inside the insurance core system
- No AI/ML features
:::

# Your worry: months spent decoding the core system

| What went wrong before | Our safeguard |
|---|---|
| Partners spent months deciphering the core insurance environment | **Week 1 pairing:** two of your core-system experts walk us through the data (2 half-days); we write a glossary from your spec sheets, not from guesses |
| Hub and link patterns implemented wrongly | **Data Vault architect leads the modelling** (CDVP2); pattern review by your warehouse owner before each increment |
| Slow ramp-up burns cycles | **Ramp-up is inside the fixed price.** A gate at end of week 2: if we are not productive, you stop with 8 days spent |
| Blueprints drifting | Blueprint conformance checklist per increment, signed off by you |

> Honest unknown: the core-system extraction interface and data quality are known to you, not to us. Week 1 profiling settles it; the week-2 gate decides scope.
