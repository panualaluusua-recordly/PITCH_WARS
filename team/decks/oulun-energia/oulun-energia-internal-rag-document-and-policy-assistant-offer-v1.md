# Test a Finnish-language assistant before winter operations depend on it
## Eight-week bounded pilot · €31,360 excl. VAT · Q1 production freeze respected

For 260 employees, retrieve answers from agreed safety, policy and operating documents with source citations and Finnish evaluation. Pilot one document domain in non-production; decide after measured quality, access and cost checks whether to expand.

---

# Ground answers in approved documents and permissions

Approved documents → incremental index → access-filtered retrieval → Finnish answer with source, version and date → existing chat tool. Weak evidence produces abstention and referral to the document owner. No autonomous operational actions or training on customer documents.

Refresh only changed content. Agree per-run and monthly caps; alert before threshold and pause refresh automatically at the cap. Report spend per run and month. Public cloud, chat and current data setup are confirmed in discovery; no new platform assumed.

---

# Evaluate Finnish quality and safety before each release gate

| Weeks | Work and gate |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Inventory one document domain; confirm cloud, chat, access model, Q1 freeze and baseline costs. Go/no-go on source quality and permissions. |
| 3–5 | Build non-production retrieval slice, citation/version display and capped incremental refresh. |
| 6–7 | Operations staff assess Finnish questions, citations, stale/ambiguous documents and cases requiring abstention. |
| 8 | Report quality, access tests and spend; deliver runbook and post-Q1 rollout decision. |

Targets are set after baseline, not invented in advance. No production changes during Q1 freeze; release only in a buyer-approved window.

---

# €31,360 covers one pilot slice and measured handover

**Team:** proposed AI/data engineer 24 days × €960 and solution architect 8 days × €1,040. Buyer document owners and operations users validate the test set. Names, credentials and actual availability require roster and owner confirmation.

**Total:** **€31,360 excl. VAT**, fixed for one document domain, test environment and eight-week plan. Buyer provides access, documents, chat/cloud credentials and model choice; cloud/model consumption and new licences excluded. No overage or scope expansion without written approval.

**Risks:** source quality, permissions, Finnish terminology and Q1 freeze dates are unverified. Workshops and runbook in Finnish, subject to language-capable staffing confirmation. No contractual deviations proposed pending standard terms review.
