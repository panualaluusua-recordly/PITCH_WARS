# Prove one supply-chain forecast before expanding
## Six-week bounded pilot · €27,600 excl. VAT · existing Azure Databricks

Metsä Group wants demand and inventory forecasts for pulp, board and wood products. We propose testing one agreed product family and hub, then deciding from measured forecast quality and operating effort whether to extend. No enterprise transformation blueprint.

---

# Start with the existing SAP-to-Databricks flow

SAP S/4HANA feeds → existing Azure Databricks data → baseline and candidate forecast → BI output → planner review.

First compare a mature commercial package with a simple baseline. Build one candidate only if evidence shows a gap. No custom algorithm becomes a supported product without an explicit maintenance comparison. Forecasts advise planners; they do not trigger production or replenishment automatically.

---

# A week-one gate protects the remaining effort

| Week | Work and decision |
|---|---|
| 1 | Agree product family, hub, horizon and planning decision; inspect history and access. Stop if data cannot support fair evaluation. |
| 2–4 | Establish baseline; compare package option and one candidate on the agreed slice. |
| 5 | Back-test by relevant segment; planners review usefulness and exceptions. |
| 6 | Deliver BI-ready pilot output, runbook, measured operating effort and scale/no-scale recommendation. |

Deliverables: documented scope and data findings, reproducible evaluation, forecast comparison, planner review log and next-step estimate. Error targets are set after the baseline, not asserted in advance.

---

# €27,600 buys a measured decision

Proposed data scientist (18 days × €1,040) and data engineer (12 days × €920): **€27,600 excl. VAT**. Day rates meet the stated AI/data role floors; price owner to confirm role classification. Names, credentials, Finnish delivery and availability require roster evidence before commitment.

Assumes one hub, one product family, agreed horizon, timely SAP/Databricks and BI access, and planner participation. New licences, full rollout and production automation are excluded. No overage without written approval. Bi-weekly written updates; pairing with the internal engineering team. No contractual deviations proposed, subject to buyer terms review.

Measures: baseline and candidate forecast error, planner usefulness/override reasons, and observed run/maintenance effort. No euro savings or payback claimed without buyer cost and inventory inputs.
