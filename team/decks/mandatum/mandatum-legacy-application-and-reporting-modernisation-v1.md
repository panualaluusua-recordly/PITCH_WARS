# No script touches live data without a person approving it

## Your committee remembers a script that pushed changes straight into live pipelines. Our answer is structural.

| Control | How it works |
|---|---|
| **No direct writes to production** | Nothing of ours has write access to live pipelines. Changes go through a reviewed change request |
| **Human approval per business line** | Life insurance and asset management reporting each have a named Mandatum approver. Automation prepares and tests; a person decides |
| **Test first, roll back always** | Every change runs in a test copy, with row-count and reconciliation checks, and has a tested rollback |
| **Audit trail** | Who proposed, reviewed and approved each change is logged for your compliance committee |

> Policies, claims and pension data stay under today's approval rules. Automation recommends and checks; it never decides.
