| Your concern | Our answer |

| Work halts at procurement | Any extension is yours to approve; we never stop silently |
| Ownership after we leave | Your team co-writes every document; AMS partner joins at the handover workshop |

> Unknown: model count and data quality. Discovery counts them in two weeks; the gate resets Phase 2.
