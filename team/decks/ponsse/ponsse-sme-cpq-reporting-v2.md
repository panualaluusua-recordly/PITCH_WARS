# Recommended solution: one reconciled source for executive KPIs

| Step | What happens | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Map the feeds** | Quotes, customer contracts and machine production data in Dynamics 365 and the current data setup are mapped field by field | Every field has a source, owner and definition |
| **2 · Align definitions** | Machine metrics and group-ledger definitions agreed with Finance in a one-page glossary | Finance signs off each definition |
| **3 · Reconcile automatically** | Machine production and delivery figures matched to ledger postings on every load | Differences flagged by order and machine, so controllers see them **before month-end close** |
| **4 · Executive dashboard** | 6–8 KPIs in your BI tool, each drillable down to the ledger entry | KPI list confirmed in week 1 |

**Out of scope:** the sales-function reporting (decided at the gate), new tools, AI features, replacing Dynamics 365.

---

# Rollout plan: assess first, commit later

| Phase | When | What | Gate or result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 · Assessment** | Weeks 1–2 | Profile the quote, contract and machine feeds on real data; map gaps; draft glossary | **Go / no-go.** Written findings: what is clean, what is a mess, what it costs to fix |
| **1 · Executive dashboard** | Weeks 3–8 | Build steps 3 and 4 on the feeds that passed; pair with your people | First executive dashboard replaces one monthly Excel report |
| **Later** | After week 8 | Sales-function reporting, scoped and priced separately **only if you want it** | Not part of this price |

> **Honest unknown:** the state of the quote and contract feeds. We can't know it before Phase 0. If they are worse than expected, we tell you at the gate and propose a smaller scope instead of building on sand.
