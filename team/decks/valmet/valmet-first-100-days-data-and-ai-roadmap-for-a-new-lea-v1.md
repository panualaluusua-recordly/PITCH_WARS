# Proposed solution and engagement methodology

:::cols
### How we work
- **Evidence first, inside your Snowflake.** We read what is already there: Snowflake, the Infor CloudSuite extracts that land in it, and the core reports people use. No new tools, no data copied out, no new US platform.
- **Working demo on your real data at the week-3 gate:** one Infor-to-report metric traced end to end (source field, Snowflake table, report), with its lineage and its mismatches
- **Every priority carries a plain-language reason:** "report X and Infor disagree by 3.1% because of cut-off timing"
- **Adaptive scope:** each gate re-ranks what we study next
:::col
### Output: the 100-day roadmap
- Days 1-30: quick wins on existing data and reports
- Days 31-60: Infor-to-Snowflake-to-reporting gaps closed
- Days 61-100: AI and data use cases, ranked by value and effort
- Team set-up, stakeholder map, risks
- **Process-modelling position:** start with transparent baselines and rules; statistical models only where they beat them on your data; physics-based models as a roadmap option
- **Out of scope:** building platforms, dashboards, production changes
:::

# Past lessons answered

| What went wrong before | Our safeguard |
|---|---|
| **Too much client pairing** | **Your time is capped at 4 h per week:** one 2 h review plus chat questions. No pairing, nobody pulled off shifts. We work alone between reviews. |
| **Q1 change freeze ignored** | We only read data and write to a sandbox schema, so **no production change is needed**. Every roadmap go-live is dated **after the Q1 freeze**. Week 1 confirms the freeze dates. |
| **Process-modelling position missing** | Stated on the previous slide: transparent first, statistical where proven, physics-based as an option. |
| **Locked scope hides what matters** | Two gates re-prioritise the work. We drop what the data shows is low value. |

> Honest unknown: we do not yet know how Infor CloudSuite data reaches Snowflake (which extracts, how often, how complete). The week-1 inventory answers this, and Gate 1 decides how deep the roadmap can go.
