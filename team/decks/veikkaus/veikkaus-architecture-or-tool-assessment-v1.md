# Technical methodology and deliverables

## Four lenses, one rule: every finding carries its evidence

| # | Deliverable (Finnish + Swedish) | How we build it, and the evidence behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Current-state map and licence-utilisation baseline** | Architecture map from configs and metadata; entitlement vs. usage per multi-year commitment from admin and usage logs |
| **D2** | **Technical-debt register, ranked** | Pipeline and model inventory scanned for failure rate, manual steps, duplicated logic, undocumented jobs; each item with counts from task logs |
| **D3** | **Model deployment workflow review** | Lead time from trained model to production measured from deployment history; every manual hand-off and approval step listed |
| **D4** | **Target-state patterns (2–3 options)** | Each scored on compliance, throughput, effort and licence reuse; recommendation **uses existing capacity first** |
| **D5** | **Roadmap and decision memo** | Sequenced, with effort in days and owners; one page for management |

> **Finding card (sample brought to the interview):** Finding · Evidence (log extract, count, date) · Impact · Effort · Confidence high / medium / low · Compliance note.
